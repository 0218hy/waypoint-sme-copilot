# Waypoint: UX and market research memo

19 Oct 2026. Desk research only; no interviews. **[unverified]** marks claims we could not confirm in a primary source. Our own measures come from `results/RESULTS.md` (E1a: median **30** owner turns, 5 of them single taps accepting quote lines, 37 typed answers not understood). Live replies currently take 8–12 s.

## A. Conversational UX for financial onboarding

- **Length drives drop-off.** Finance applications have high abandonment: 68% of European consumers abandoned one in 2021, up from 63% in 2020. The top reasons were that it took too long, asked for too much data, and 30% found it "complicated" ([Signicat, 2022](https://www.signicat.com/press-releases/the-battle-to-onboard-2022); older than 2023). Across forms, the longer a form takes, the fewer people finish it, and mobile starters finish less often than desktop ones (47.5% vs 55.5%) ([Zuko benchmarks, 2025](https://www.zuko.io/benchmarking/industry-benchmarking)). 76% of SMEs rank a short application among their top three criteria for choosing finance ([Mambu, 2022](https://ffnews.com/news/mambu-two-thirds-of-smes-unable-to-secure-funding-due-to-limited-access-and-choice-in-business-lending); older). We found **no credible "N questions before drop-off" number** for chat. Treat any figure like that as unverified.
- **Chat by itself is not a win.** Controlled studies are mixed. A chatbot survey gave no better data than a web form ([Zarouali et al., 2023](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/367098888_Comparing_Chatbots_and_Online_Surveys_for_Longitudinal_Data_Collection_An_Investigation_of_Response_Characteristics_Data_Quality_and_User_Evaluation)). A 2026 randomized experiment found no gain in speed and found the chat design **harder to navigate** ([Çavuşoğlu Deveci et al., 2026](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/07591063261424249)). The benefit comes from asking fewer, better questions, not from the chat format.
- **Quick replies vs free text.** Choice-based answers produced clearer client input, but users rated them as less dependable (n=50 experiment) ([arXiv 2506.11610, Jun 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11610); preprint). WeChat's official design guide says "做选择而不是键盘输入" (choose rather than type) and asks designers to cut typing with camera input and history shortcuts ([WeChat mini-program design guide](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/design/); undated, official). Taps should be the default, with free text kept available.
- **Value before effort.** Duolingo moved sign-up behind a first lesson and reports about a 20% DAU lift ([First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/the-tenets-of-a-b-testing-from-duolingos-master-growth-hacker); company-reported, date unverified). MYbank's 百灵 2.0 starts from one sentence ("想把额度提一提", roughly "I'd like a higher limit") and reaches a full plan in about 10 minutes, against 3 days before ([Tide News, 11 Sep 2026](https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3555854)).
- **Latency.** At about 1 s a user's flow of thought breaks, and at about 10 s their attention is lost; beyond that, show progress ([NN/g response-time limits](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/response-times-3-important-limits); classic). A CHI 2026 study fixed time-to-first-token at 2, 9 or 20 s. Behaviour did not change, users read the delay as the AI "thinking", and long waits sometimes turned into frustration ([Tan et al., CHI 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06183)). For chatbots, short delays scaled to the message helped novices but put off experienced users ([Gnewuch et al., BISE 2022](https://aisel.aisnet.org/bise/vol64/iss6/5)). WeChat recommends **local** loading feedback on the part being loaded, not a full-page spinner, plus a cancel option for long waits ([WeChat design guide](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/design/)). For us this means: a deliberative pause is acceptable for "here is your plan", but not for confirming a tap.
- **Voice.** We found no 2023–26 data on voice use by Chinese SME owners **[unverified]**. Older data: input-method users made about 10 voice entries a day in 2020 ([iiMedia via DoNews, 2020](https://www.donews.com/news/detail/4/3097233)), and about 16% of WeChat messages were voice ([Statista via iFanr, c. 2018](https://www.ifanr.com/1152462)). Hold-to-talk is a familiar habit and worth offering, but nothing shows it should be the main input.
- **WeChat conventions.** One clear focus per page; don't interrupt the user's goal; touch targets of about 7–9 mm; reuse standard components ([WeChat design guide](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/design/)).

## B. What Chinese F&B brands in Singapore actually struggle with

- **Crowding.** About 85 Chinese F&B brands ran about 405 outlets in Aug 2025, against 32 brands and 184 outlets in Jun 2024 (Momentum Works via [Reuters/Inside Retail, 13 Oct 2025](https://insideretail.asia/2025/10/13/chinese-fb-chains-are-using-singapore-as-a-testbed-for-global-expansion/)). Knight Frank now sees "measured growth driven by quality" ([Q4 2025](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/531/documents/en/singapore-retail-market-update-q4-2025-12618.pdf)). More than 3,000 F&B outlets closed in 2024, the most in about 20 years, and about 300 a month closed in 2025 ([ASEAN Briefing, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.aseanbriefing.com/news/singapores-fb-sector-entering-2026-an-entry-decision-for-foreign-investors-cn/)). Of retail food establishments that closed between 1 Jan and 23 Oct 2025, 63% had been registered for five years or less, and 82% of those never declared a profit (checked against the official [MTI written reply, 5 Nov 2025](https://www.mti.gov.sg/newsroom/written-reply-to-pq-on-retail-food-establishments-which-ceased-operations-after-five-years-or-less-that-have-recorded-profit), checked 19 Oct 2026).
- **Rent.** One chain operator reported rents 30%+ above 2022, and Colliers had Q3 2024 retail rents up 2.7% y/y ([Huxiu, 23 Oct 2024](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3608403.html)). A tenants' group says members report renewal increases of 20–49% ([Vulcan Post, 2025](https://vulcanpost.com/912319/why-singapores-fb-giants-are-retreating-from-china/)).
- **Manpower: the biggest gap in our flow.** In services, foreign workers may be at most **35%** of the workforce, S Pass holders at most 10%, and **PRC Work Permit holders at most 8%**. Quota is earned from the **3-month average of local CPF-paid staff** ([MOM, updated 19 Feb 2026](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy/levy-and-quota-requirements)). So a new shop can't bring a Shenzhen crew, and it must hire locals first. Taiyi's Singapore stores run about 20 staff against 25–30 in China. The F&B minimum wage reached S$2,080 in 2025 ([Huxiu, 2024](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3608403.html)). A manager on an Employment Pass needs at least S$5,600 a month from Jan 2025, plus a COMPASS points score ([EDB](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/news-and-insights/salary-threshold-for-new-employment-pass-applicants-to-be-raised-to-5600-from-2025)).
- **Licensing.** SFA gives in-principle approval for a food shop licence in about 7 working days. Renovation can't start before that approval, and the inspection comes after fit-out ([GoBusiness](https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/licensing-faqs/food-shop-licence)). Importing tea or ingredients for sale needs SFA licensing or registration ([SFA](https://sfa.gov.sg/food-import-export/commercial-food-imports)). Almost everything is imported ([Huxiu, 2024](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3608403.html)).
- **Partners and structure.** Chagee closed its franchised Singapore shops in Jan 2024 amid reported franchisee problems, then returned in Aug 2024 under direct management ([EqualOcean, Jan 2024](https://equalocean.com/news/2024012220464-chinese-new-style-tea-chagee-renames-all-singapore-stores-amidst-franchisee); [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/business/companies/chagee-mixue-and-luckin-coffee-expand-despite-wave-of-closures-in-singapore-s-f-b-scene-4995003.html)).
- **Landlord vetting.** We found **no credible source** on how malls screen new-to-market brands (track record, proposals, guarantees) **[unverified]**. Deposits are often taken partly at the letter of offer, and some landlords accept a banker's guarantee instead of cash. UOB's collateral-free guarantee needs 30% local equity ([UOB/SMRT](https://www.uob.com.sg/business/sme-hub/insights/smrt-tenants.page); undated).
- **ODI timing.** We found no official processing times for the NDRC, MOFCOM and SAFE-bank steps ([Zhong Lun](https://www.zhonglun.com/research/articles/7447.html)) **[unverified]**.

**Not covered by our five steps (by importance):** (1) the staffing plan and quota: local hiring has to come before foreign hires and before opening, which affects both cost and date; (2) a "hire locals" milestone; (3) SFA import registration for the tea supply chain; (4) the choice between a partner or franchise and running the shop directly; (5) how a mall screens and accepts a brand new to Singapore.

## D. Simplicity recommendations (ranked by impact/effort)

Turn savings are our estimates against E1a and should be re-measured. None of these changes drops a legal step, the WeBank-loan block, owner approval of money steps, or code-computed numbers.

1. **Taps never wait for the model.** After a tap, show a scripted line and the code-computed card within 1 s, then stream the voice text in afterwards. Call the model only for free text that code can't place. *Measured: 8–12 s replies; [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/response-times-3-important-limits), [CHI 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06183).* Impact high, effort low.
2. **"Accept all" for quote lines.** Accept all read lines in one tap, and list only the uncertain ones for review. *Measured: 5 taps.* About −4 turns. Low effort.
3. **Cut questions to defaults.** Drop "areas to rule out", "unit size" (take it from his shop format), "fit-out level" (default: standard), "emails show amounts" (default: no) and "check-in frequency" (default: weekly). He can change any of them on the card or the Account tab. *[Zuko 2025](https://www.zuko.io/benchmarking/industry-benchmarking), [Signicat](https://www.signicat.com/press-releases/the-battle-to-onboard-2022).* About −5 turns. Low effort.
4. **Start from one sentence, a voice note or a photo.** Parse several profile slots at once, then show `profile_card` to confirm. *[百灵 2.0](https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3555854); measured: 37 typed answers not understood.* About −3 turns. Medium effort.
5. **A ballpark card after about 3 answers.** Show an early code-computed range: "a small mall kiosk: S$X–Y upfront, opening around month N". *[Duolingo](https://review.firstround.com/the-tenets-of-a-b-testing-from-duolingos-master-growth-hacker).* Medium effort.
6. **Merge "pick unit" with "use this estimate".** `unit_compare` already shows the early cost, so one tap picks the unit and accepts the estimate, and quotes refine it later. About −2 turns. Low effort.
7. **Add staffing as a cost line, a topic and a milestone.** Cover the PRC Work Permit 8% sub-quota, the local-first hiring rule and the S$2,080 wage floor, so the dates are honest. *[MOM 2026](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/quota-and-levy/levy-and-quota-requirements).* No turns added if it is raised as a topic. Medium effort.
8. **Plain words.** Use 境外投资备案 rather than "ODI", "if things go badly" rather than P95, "rent tied to sales" rather than GTO, and keep definitions behind a tap. *[Signicat](https://www.signicat.com/press-releases/the-battle-to-onboard-2022): 30% found applications "complicated".* Low effort.
9. **Make waits honest and local.** Show a placeholder skeleton in the card being computed and "Working out your dates…" for the long plan step, never a full-screen spinner. *[WeChat guide](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/design/).* Low effort.
10. **One-tap Goal Keeper emails.** Add an "On plan as usual" link, and only open chat when something differs. *Measured: E6 3.9 false alerts per chain-year; raise the alert thresholds as well.* Medium effort.

Together, items 2–4 and 6 are worth about 14 turns, which would bring the median from 30 to roughly 16.
